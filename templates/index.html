Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How do I book an appointment?

Simply click "Book Now" and fill out our online form. You'll receive a reference code and a confirmation call within 24 hours.

What should I do before my appointment?

Come with clean skin if possible, avoid heavy makeup, and stay hydrated. For peels, avoid sun exposure 48 hours prior.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes! You can cancel up to 24 hours before your appointment through our "My Bookings" page or by calling us.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes, we offer complimentary 15-minute consultations. For detailed skin analysis, our 30-minute consultation starts at $75.
